01

The shift you noticed is real

Heavy, grind-only training is losing its monopoly. The growth in 2026 is in formats that build strength and keep the body mobile, joint-friendly and recoverable. Every major chain is reacting to it.

Trend

Hybrid over heavy

Hyrox-style training (run + functional stations) is the fastest-growing search term in fitness, up over 120% year on year. Delhi's second Hyrox event pulled 2,600+ athletes, 50% more than the Mumbai debut.

Trend

Low-impact strength

Reformer Pilates, mobility circuits and eccentric training are the year's headline formats. F45 launched FS8 (Pilates + tone + yoga) and Solidcore added a heated strength class. Even big-box chains like Crunch are adding 10+ reformer studios.

Trend

Recovery is a product

Cold plunge, infrared sauna and compression boots have moved from elite-athlete rooms into premium Indian gyms and are now sold as standalone bookable sessions. Orangetheory's CEO named recovery and stretch classes as the next expansion.

What this means for VAULT: do not compete on "more machines". Compete on departments (skills people cannot get at Cult) and on recovery (a wing nobody in the city has). That is what "a level above Anytime and Cult" actually looks like.
02

New exercises and formats to run

Mapped to your six questions. "Hot" is what is exploding now, "New" is what is just arriving in India, "Core" is what the floor cannot work without.

Hot

Hyrox simulation

  • 8 × 1 km run + 8 stations: SkiErg, sled push, sled pull, burpee broad jumps, rowing, farmers carry, sandbag lunges, wall balls
  • Weekly "Vault Race" class, monthly timed sim, team of 2 doubles format
  • Register as an official Hyrox affiliate gym (Jetts just opened the first Hyrox Training Club in Vasant Kunj, Delhi)
Hot

Reformer Pilates

  • 6 to 10 reformers in a 40–60 sqm enclosed room
  • Formats: Reformer Strength, Reformer Flow (mobility), Athlete Reformer for lifters
  • Biggest male growth format of 2026 alongside Hyrox and calisthenics
New

Adult gymnastics

  • Handstands, rings, bridges, tumbling basics on a sprung floor
  • Gyms offering adult classes in the US went from 231 (2015) to 590 after the 2024 Olympics
  • Class formula that works: 15 min strength circuit, 30 min flexibility, 15 min floor skills
New

Martial arts

  • Boxing fundamentals, Muay Thai pads, no-gi grappling / BJJ, MMA conditioning
  • Beginner-first programming; belt-free "skills ladder" so gym members are not intimidated
  • Martial arts academies are one of the three boutique formats urban Indians are moving to, alongside climbing and specialised yoga
Core

Calisthenics + Animal Flow

  • Ring and bar skills, levers, muscle-ups; "Calisthenics rings" hit its all-time search peak in Jan–Mar 2026
  • Animal Flow and ground-based mobility as a daily 30-minute class
  • Needs an outdoor-style rig indoors, not a cable machine
Core

Mobility and eccentric strength

  • Loaded stretching, Jefferson curls, ATG split squats, Nordic curls, tibialis work
  • "Knees Over Toes" style longevity strength for the 30–50 age group
  • Daily 7 am and 7 pm mobility class is the easiest retention tool in the building
From abroad

Strength-cardio fusion classes

  • Barry's model: treadmill intervals + heavy floor strength in 50 minutes
  • Solidcore model: slow, heated, 50-minute resistance class
  • Interval walking and incline "12-3-30" style low-impact cardio for the recovery-minded
From abroad

Racquet and run club

  • Padel and pickleball are the two fastest-growing social sports; even UK gyms are pairing padel with reformer studios
  • If land allows, one covered padel court is the strongest social hook in the city
  • Weekly VAULT run club (Saturday 6 am) feeds the Hyrox pipeline
From abroad

Kettlebell, rucking, sandbag

  • Kettlebell flow and StrongFirst-style technique classes
  • Rucking (weighted walks) as a Sunday community event
  • Sandbag and odd-object strength ties directly into Hyrox stations

04

The six departments

This is the new structure premium clubs are moving to. Instead of "trainers on a floor", each department has a head coach, its own programme and its own member events.

1 · Strength & Physique

Classic lifting, hypertrophy, powerlifting club, PT. The base that every member expects.

2 · Hybrid & Hyrox

Race training, run club, engine building. Head coach runs the monthly simulation and takes a team to real Hyrox events in Mumbai, Delhi, Bangalore.

3 · Movement & Skills

Adult gymnastics, calisthenics, handstands, Animal Flow. The department that gives VAULT an identity Cult cannot copy fast.

4 · Combat

Boxing, Muay Thai, grappling, self-defence for women, kids' martial arts on weekends (brings in families).

5 · Pilates & Mobility

Reformer, mat, yoga, loaded stretching, prehab. The low-impact department for the 30–55 age group with the highest spending power.

6 · Recovery & Performance

Sauna, cold plunge, compression, red light, body composition and VO2 testing, nutrition coaching. Sold both inside membership and as day passes to non-members.

06

Design language

Palette

Near-black base, warm charcoal, one metallic accent (brass or gold) and raw concrete. Avoid the neon green/orange of volume gyms. A premium club looks like a hotel gym, not a stadium.

Light

Three moods in one building: cool and bright on the strength floor, warm and dim in the reformer and mobility studios, near-dark with amber in recovery. Lighting is zoned, not uniform.

Sound

Each studio has its own system. Acoustic panels on the turf and strength walls so the Pilates room stays silent. This single detail separates premium from mid-market.

Materials

Timber, stone, black steel, leather seating in the lounge. Branded hardware (racks, rigs, reformers) in one colour so the floor reads as one system.

Space

Negative space between equipment clusters. Semi-private PT pods. Wide circulation so classes can move between turf and strength without crossing lifters.

Arrival

Reception doubles as a café and protein bar. Members walk past the recovery wing's glass wall on the way in: the product sells itself.

Membership tiers

Tier 1

Floor

Strength floor, cardio deck, functional turf, daily mobility class. Priced just above Anytime Fitness.

Tier 2

Club

Everything in Floor plus all department classes (Hyrox, Combat, Movement, Reformer), quarterly testing, 4 recovery sessions a month.

Tier 3

Vault

Unlimited recovery, 2 PT sessions a month, nutrition coaching, race-team entry, guest passes, priority booking. Limited to a capped number of members.

Plus day passes for recovery and drop-in Hyrox sims so non-members taste the premium side first, which is how most premium clubs now convert.

08

Roll-out plan

Lock the zones

Finalise floor plan with recovery and reformer rooms drawn first (they have the hardest MEP and acoustic needs). Check floor load for plunges.

Hire the six heads

One head coach per department. The Movement and Combat heads are the hardest to find; start early.

Equip in this order

Strength and turf (revenue day one), then reformers and recovery (differentiators), then combat and gymnastics rig.

Launch events

Open with a Hyrox simulation, a free recovery weekend and a handstand workshop. Apply for Hyrox affiliate status.

Measure

Track class fill rate per department monthly. Expand what fills, cut what does not, within 90 days.
